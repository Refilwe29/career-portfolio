
# Agile Team Charter – PowerShare

## 1. Project Overview

**Project Name:** PowerShare  
**Methodology:** Agile  
**Target Users:** Micro-entrepreneurs in Khayelitsha and Mitchells Plain

### Project Purpose

PowerShare is an affordable, pay-as-you-go portable energy rental service designed to help micro-entrepreneurs continue operating during power outages.

Frequent load shedding, load reduction and infrastructure failures can cause outages lasting two to four hours during peak trading times. These interruptions affect businesses such as barbershops, tailoring businesses and spaza shops, resulting in lost customers, reduced daily income and difficulties paying staff and supporting families.

PowerShare aims to reduce these losses by providing portable lithium power stations that can keep essential business equipment running during power interruptions.

## 2. Project Goals

The main goals of PowerShare are to:

- Reduce business interruptions caused by power outages.
- Provide an affordable and accessible energy rental service.
- Support small businesses in maintaining daily operations.
- Help entrepreneurs protect their income during outages.
- Make portable power more accessible through a pay-as-you-go model.
- Develop a solution that responds to the needs of the target users.
- Use feedback and testing to improve the proposed solution.

## 3. Target Users and Their Needs

The primary target users are micro-entrepreneurs operating in Khayelitsha and Mitchells Plain.

Examples include:

- **Barbers:** Need electricity for clippers, lighting and other essential equipment.
- **Tailors:** Need electricity for sewing machines and work lighting.
- **Spaza shop owners:** May need backup power for essential lighting, payment devices and other compatible equipment.

These entrepreneurs depend on daily cash flow to support their households and, where applicable, pay their employees. PowerShare aims to help them continue essential business activities when the electricity supply is interrupted.

The exact equipment supported will depend on the capacity and compatibility of the selected power station.

## 4. Team Goals and Responsibilities

The PowerShare team aims to work collaboratively to plan, develop and evaluate a solution that meets the needs of its intended users.

Team members should:

- Understand the problem and the needs of the target users.
- Identify and prioritise the project's requirements.
- Break larger requirements into smaller, manageable tasks.
- Complete assigned work within agreed deadlines.
- Test the solution and review feedback.
- Maintain project documentation and evidence of progress.

## 5. Team Values

The team agrees to follow these values:

- **Collaboration:** Work together and share ideas.
- **Respect:** Listen to different opinions and treat everyone fairly.
- **Accountability:** Take responsibility for assigned tasks.
- **Transparency:** Communicate progress, delays and challenges honestly.
- **User focus:** Keep the needs of small-business owners at the centre of decisions.
- **Continuous improvement:** Use feedback to improve the solution.
- **Quality:** Ensure that project work meets the agreed requirements.

## 6. Working Agreements

The PowerShare team agrees to:

1. Communicate regularly about project progress.
2. Assign clear responsibilities for project tasks.
3. Set realistic deadlines and monitor completion.
4. Update the task board when work starts or is completed.
5. Raise challenges early so the team can find solutions.
6. Attend agreed meetings and contribute to planning.
7. Review work and provide constructive feedback.
8. Keep project files, decisions and evidence organised.
9. Prioritise features according to user needs and project requirements.

## 7. Agile Approach

PowerShare follows an Agile approach by organising project work into smaller tasks and improving the solution through ongoing planning, development, testing and feedback.

The product backlog records and prioritises the features and requirements needed for the project. The sprint backlog identifies the tasks selected for a particular sprint. Regular progress reviews help the team identify challenges, adjust priorities and ensure that development remains focused on the needs of micro-entrepreneurs.

## 9. Personal Reflection

Through the PowerShare project, I have developed a better understanding of how Agile teamwork can help address a real-world problem. The project focuses on micro-entrepreneurs who may lose income when power outages interrupt their daily business activities.

I have learned that a successful project requires more than developing a solution. It also requires understanding users, setting clear goals, communicating effectively and dividing work into manageable tasks.

The team charter helps establish shared expectations and provides guidance on how the team should collaborate. It also reminds me that project decisions should focus on the actual needs of the people who will use the solution.

## 10. Conclusion

PowerShare aims to help micro-entrepreneurs in Khayelitsha and Mitchells Plain maintain essential business activities during power outages through affordable, pay-as-you-go portable energy rentals.

This team charter establishes the principles and working agreements that guide the project. By applying Agile practices, maintaining communication and prioritising user needs, the team can work towards a practical and valuable solution.
