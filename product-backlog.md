# PowerShare – Product Backlog

## 1. Introduction

The PowerShare product backlog is a prioritised list of features and requirements for the proposed pay-as-you-go portable energy rental service. It focuses on helping micro-entrepreneurs in Khayelitsha and Mitchells Plain access backup power during electricity outages.

## 2. Product Backlog Items

| ID | User Story / Feature | Priority | Story Points |
|---|---|---|---:|
| P1 | View available power stations | High | 5 |
| P2 | Rent a power station | High | 8 |
| P3 | View rental prices | High | 5 |
| P4 | Book a power station | High | 8 |
| P5 | Process rental payments | High | 8 |
| P6 | View power outage and availability information | High | 5 |
| P7 | Check rental request status | Medium | 5 |
| P8 | Manage power station availability | Medium | 5 |
| P9 | Manage customer bookings | Medium | 5 |
| P10 | View instructions on using PowerShare | Medium | 3 |
| P11 | Access customer support information | Medium | 3 |
| P12 | View information about PowerShare | Low | 2 |
| P13 | View terms and conditions | Low | 3 |

*Story points are initial estimates for planning and should be reviewed by the team.*

## 3. Prioritisation

### High Priority

These features support the core service: finding available power stations, understanding rental costs and requesting a rental.

### Medium Priority

These features improve rental management, customer communication and the overall user experience.

### Low Priority

These features provide supporting information that is useful but less critical to the initial rental process.

## 4. Backlog Management

The backlog should be reviewed regularly as the team learns more about user needs and project requirements.

The team should:
- Add new requirements when necessary.
- Update priorities when business needs change.
- Break large features into smaller tasks.
- Estimate the effort required for each item.
- Select suitable items for each sprint.
- Track progress using the team's chosen project management tool.

## 6. Personal Reflection

Creating the PowerShare product backlog helped me understand how Agile teams organise requirements according to their importance. Features such as viewing available power stations, understanding prices and requesting rentals are important because they support the main purpose of the service.

I also learned that prioritisation helps a team focus on essential features before spending time on less critical improvements. Estimating story points can support sprint planning, although estimates may change as the team gains more information.

## 7. Conclusion

The PowerShare product backlog provides an organised starting point for development. It helps the team understand the required features, prioritise work and prepare for sprint planning while keeping the needs of small-business owners in focus.
