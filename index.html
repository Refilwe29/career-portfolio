A little about this portfolio

Organised work, clear evidence and honest reflection.

Welcome! This portfolio brings together five Agile assessment sections for PowerShare, a proposed pay-as-you-go portable energy rental service intended to support micro-entrepreneurs during power outages.

Each section explains a different part of Agile planning. Supporting screenshots should be genuine project evidence, and reflections should describe my actual learning and contributions.

My Learning Journey

Preparing this portfolio has helped me explore how Agile artefacts support teamwork, requirements analysis and project planning. I am learning to distinguish planned work from completed work and to use evidence to support what I report.

My aim is to keep improving how I document decisions, track progress, reflect on challenges and organise project work.
